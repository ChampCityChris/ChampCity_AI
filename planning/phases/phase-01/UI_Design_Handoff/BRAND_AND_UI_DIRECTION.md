# Brand And UI Direction

## Product Name

ChampCity A/I

## Meaning Of A/I

`A/I` is a play on AI, but means `Architect / Implementer`.

The product embodies the ChatGPT / Codex dichotomy:

- Architect frames, questions, reviews, and translates intent.
- Implementer builds from a structured handoff.

## Tone

The UI should be:

- Compact.
- Modern.
- Dark.
- Consumer-friendly.
- Polished.
- Calm.
- Confident.
- Approachable for non-developer Operators.

## Avoid

Avoid:

- Ticketing-system look.
- Enterprise admin dashboard look.
- Giant forms.
- Developer-console aesthetic.
- Raw Markdown-first appearance.
- Overly playful game UI.
- Dense bureaucracy.
- Visual choices that make the product feel like internal tooling.

## Prefer

Prefer:

- Guided cockpit.
- Elegant dark panels.
- Clear progress.
- Compact cards.
- Soft contrast.
- Readable typography.
- Consumer SaaS polish.
- Subtle brand personality.
- Plain-language labels.
- Clear status and save feedback.
- Artifact previews that feel designed.

## Design Principle

Structured planning should feel approachable, not bureaucratic. The app should help the Operator feel that they are steering a creative build process with an Architect and an Implementer, not filling out internal tickets.

## UI Implications

- Use dark surfaces with enough contrast for long reading sessions.
- Treat workflow stages as a guided loop, not a flat nav list.
- Make warnings and validation errors visible without making the app feel hostile.
- Let long artifacts collapse, preview, or sit in focused panels.
- Keep field labels functionally recognizable for Codex implementation.
- Use compact responsive desktop layouts suitable for Electron.
- Preserve all current screens and states while reducing form fatigue.
